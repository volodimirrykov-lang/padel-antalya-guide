Padel in Side — where to play and whether the trip to Antalya is worth it

Side sits about 75 km east of central Antalya. Some resort hotels have their own courts for guests; the community padel clubs with open games and coaching are in Antalya, so a padel session combines well with a day trip to the city.

Are there padel courts in Side?

Some resort hotels around Side and Manavgat have their own courts for guests — ask your hotel directly. We have not verified a public community padel club in Side itself (facts last checked on the date in the footer); the nearest community clubs are in Antalya.

Is it worth driving from Side to Antalya to play padel?

It is about 75 km to central Antalya and 85–90 km to Konyaaltı (1¼–1½ hours each way), so it works best combined with a day trip to the city. If you just want a casual hit, ask your hotel about a court first; if you want a real game with local players matched by level, or coaching, V7 Padel Antalya in Konyaaltı runs open games daily.

Can I join a padel game in Antalya without a partner?

Yes. V7 Padel Antalya in Konyaaltı runs open games and mixers where solo players are matched into a game by level. Message the club on WhatsApp (+90 546 780 0877) to join a session; the club is open 07:00–00:00 daily.

How much does a padel court cost in Antalya?

At V7 Padel Antalya a court is 2,000 TL for 60 minutes (about 500 TL per person for four players), 3,000 TL for 90 minutes and 4,000 TL for 2 hours. Hotel courts around Side are priced by each hotel — ask at reception.
