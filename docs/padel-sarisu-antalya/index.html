Padel near Sarısu — where to play on the Konyaaltı side

Sarısu sits at the western end of the Konyaaltı shoreline, and padel is a short drive along the same coast road. Here is what is available nearby, what it costs and how to get on court without a partner.

Is there a padel court in Sarısu?

Sarısu itself is a beach and picnic area rather than a sports district, so the courts sit a little inland along Konyaaltı. The nearest dedicated club is V7 Padel Antalya on Hürriyet Caddesi — 4 panoramic courts, three of them open-sided roofed and one open-air, open every day 07:00-00:00.

How far is the nearest padel club from Sarısu beach?

It is the same district: from the Sarısu side you follow the coast road east through Konyaaltı. Locals reach it in a short drive; by public transport take the tram or a Konyaaltı bus toward the city centre.

Can I play in the evening after the beach?

Yes — the club is open until midnight and evening slots between 18:00 and 22:00 are the busiest, so book a day ahead in summer. Three of the four courts are roofed, which matters in Antalya heat and in autumn rain.

What does a court cost near Sarısu?

At V7 Padel Antalya a court is 2,000 TL for 60 minutes, 3,000 TL for 90 minutes and 4,000 TL for two hours — per court, split between up to four players, so roughly 500 TL per person an hour.

I am here alone — can I still play?

Yes. The club runs open games where solo players are matched into a four by level. Message the club on WhatsApp and say your level and which days you are around.
