Padel in Belek — hotel courts and the nearest clubs

Belek is a resort area about 45 km east of central Antalya. Some resort hotels have their own padel courts for guests; for a community club with open games and coaching, the nearest options are in Antalya.

Are there padel courts in Belek?

Some Belek resort hotels have padel courts for their guests — ask your hotel directly whether it has a court and how to book it. We have not verified a public community padel club in Belek itself (facts last checked on the date in the footer); the nearest community clubs are in Antalya.

Where is the nearest padel club to Belek?

In Antalya. V7 Padel Antalya in Konyaaltı has 4 courts (3 open-sided roofed — shade and rain cover with natural airflow, not sealed air-conditioned halls — plus 1 open-air), coaching and open games; the drive from Belek is roughly 50–55 km (about an hour). On the Lara side of town, closer to Belek, Lara Padel Club has 2 outdoor courts (booking via the BallBall app).

Can I join a padel game without a partner?

Yes. V7 Padel Antalya in Konyaaltı runs open games and mixers where solo players are matched into a game by level. Message the club on WhatsApp (+90 546 780 0877) to join the next session; the club is open 07:00–00:00 daily.

How much does a padel court cost near Belek?

At V7 Padel Antalya a court is 2,000 TL for 60 minutes (about 500 TL per person for four players), 3,000 TL for 90 minutes and 4,000 TL for 2 hours. Hotel courts in Belek are priced by each hotel — ask at reception.
